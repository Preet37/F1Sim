Teach the championship about disqualification, and measure a real-sized grid

Merging main brought the 2026 qualifying classification, which makes exclusion a
state distinct from retirement: classificationTier sorts a disqualified car
behind a retired one, and CarEntry now carries both flags. The season layer only
knew about retirement, so a driver excluded from the results would have been
awarded points for wherever they were classified.

RoundResult gains a disqualified list and recordRound skips those drivers without
counting a DNF against them, because a disqualification is not a reliability
problem and the career screen shows retirements to say how reliable a car has
been. The field is optional and SAVE_MINOR goes to 2 -- an additive change, which
the codec already handles by loading anything with a higher minor and keeping
fields it does not recognise.

probe:fieldsize answers the question the design doc had been asserting rather than
testing: the real grids are 22 cars and every existing probe measures 20. The grid
holds structurally at 20, 22 and 24 -- every car starts clear, every car gets its
own pit box, everybody is classified once, the leader completes the distance. What
it costs is finishers: about 17 points of them on average, which is four more cars
arriving at the same first corner rather than a defect in the grid size.

That threshold is a catastrophe guard at 35%, not a quality bar. Encoding the
measured gap would freeze today's first-lap behaviour into a test about grid size
and fail the moment somebody improved the AI in either direction. The measurement
is printed instead, and flagged in the design doc for whoever owns racecraft.
