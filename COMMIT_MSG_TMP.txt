Make a career survive the game changing underneath it

The save was version 1 with an empty migrate() and ids pointing into the static
TEAMS and DRIVERS arrays. That works exactly as long as the grid is a constant,
and it no longer is: junior formulae, generated rookies, transfers and a hidden
per-save form roll all make it dynamic. So the world moves into the save.

Two version numbers rather than one. A newer MAJOR is refused, because guessing
at an incompatible shape corrupts a career slowly instead of failing honestly. A
newer MINOR loads, and the fields this build has never heard of are preserved
verbatim and written back out, so playing on a new build and opening the career
on an older tab does not delete anything. That works only because nothing in
CareerState is a class: an unknown key is just a value.

probe:save round-trips a ten-season career, migrates a hand-written version 1
save, and checks that eight kinds of garbage are refused distinguishably. From a
newer build and not a save at all need different things said to the player.

It also caught two things playing would not have. A career simulated straight
through ended in Formula 3 in its third season, because a rookie starts below the
field on purpose and three seasons is not enough runway; the drop rule is now
four. And the player is placed after the market closes, so a promotion took a
Formula 2 seat out of a championship that had finished filling itself, which is a
bug that only appears when the player does well.
