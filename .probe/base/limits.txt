--- The same test with the car yawed ---
A car running wide is sliding. Its four contact patches spread
across the track as it rotates, so the last one to leave the paint
is further from the centreline than half a car width. The threshold
below is metres of overlap still on the track when the lap died —
positive means the lap was deleted while the car was still on it.

--- Cars driving: how many deletions the old rule invented ---
An excursion is counted the way race control counts it: once, on the
step the car is first judged off. A FALSE one is an excursion the old
rule reported in which no part of the car ever actually left the
track. Steps are counted too, because the AI is tuned to stay inside
the line and rarely goes far past it — the player does, and the
per-step disagreement is the size of the trap they drive into.
