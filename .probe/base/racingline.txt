=== follow-the-green: does the colour keep its promise? ===

At every point on every lap, the fastest speed at which the road ahead
still reads GREEN is found, and the car is released from there: it holds
that speed for a fifth of a second — a driver reacting to the colour
CHANGING, which it has not yet done — and then brakes as hard as the
tyres allow. "worst load" is the most lateral grip it is asked for on the
way through. Above 1.00 the car leaves the road, and the green that put
it there was a lie.

Run for every car a player can actually be in, because the promise is
made to all of them and the reference car is none of them.
