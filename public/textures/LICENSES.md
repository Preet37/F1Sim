## How to regenerate

```
node scripts/generateTextures.mjs
```

The generator is deterministic — no random seed, no clock, no external input —
so a rebuild produces byte-identical files. If you change the generator, commit
the regenerated PNGs with it.

## What each one is

**`carbon_weave_normal.png`** — a tangent-space normal map of a 2/2 twill
carbon-fibre cloth, sixteen tows across the tile, tileable in both directions.
Sampled through the car's box-projected second UV set at a 100 mm tile, which
puts each tow at about six millimetres wherever it lands on the car. It carries
no lettering, no logo and no mark of any kind: it is woven cloth, which is a
generic material.

**`tyre_surface_normal.png`** — a tangent-space normal map of the moulded relief
of a racing slick, laid out to match the wheel texture atlas that
`src/render/TyreTexture.ts` builds. The lower 46 % is flat because that region
of the atlas is occupied by the rim, brake disc and caliper swatches. Above it:
circumferential tread striation, irregular graining across the working part of
the tread, the shoulder turn, and radial ribs on the sidewall. Again no
lettering and no marks — the sidewall text is painted per-compound at runtime,
in a fictional tyre-maker's name.

## Licensing boundary

This project uses fictional teams, fictional drivers and a fictional tyre
supplier deliberately. No asset in this directory, and no graphic drawn by the
livery painter in `src/render/Livery.ts`, may reproduce a real Formula One
team's livery, a real sponsor's logo, a real team or driver name, or any
Formula One trademark. Anything added here must be either generated by this
repository's own code, or CC0/public-domain from a source recorded in this
table with a link and a retrieval date.
